## Notes

- Vendor docs: OpenAI Academy/Cookbook, Anthropic Learn, Google AI Studio docs.
- Newsletters: One Useful Thing (Ethan Mollick), Ben's Bites, The Neuron, Every.
- YouTube: AI Explained, Matt Wolfe, All About AI, The AI Daily Brief.
- People: Karpathy, Willison, McLaughlin; impact: Mollick, Fei-Fei Li, Karen Hao, Crawford and others.
- Reports: Stanford HAI AI Index, OECD AI Observatory, WEF Future of Jobs, UNESCO, Our World in Data.
- PL: Radar AI (StormIT), Alzi.pl, Kamil Głusiński, GRAI (AI.gov.pl), Microsoft Learn PL, Google Cloud Skills Boost.
- Routine: 15-30 min daily newsletter, 1 practical task 2-3x/week, weekly talk, monthly review.
