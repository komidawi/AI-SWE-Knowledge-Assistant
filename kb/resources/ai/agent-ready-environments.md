## Why this one

The argument that agent output is limited by the context the environment exposes, not by model
quality — the shape of it matches how this repo is run. On-stack (`ai-tooling`), off-goal.

## Notes

1. Towards Autonomous Product Development — <https://mega.dev/autonomous-product-development>
   Give agents the app, docs, logs, dev DBs, past sessions and decisions; keep prod DB and root out.
2. Don't make the agent remember how to do its job — <https://mega.dev/pi-kody-integration>
   One prompt is not a system: a recurring job needs durable state, a schedule and a remote runtime.
