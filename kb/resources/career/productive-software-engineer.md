## Why this one

A measure-then-fix method for one's own workday, not another focus manifesto — the axis
`[[deep-work]]` does not cover. Off-goal and off-stack: never takes planned hours.

## Notes

- **Awakened Day** — log every distraction with time, emotion, stress/energy 1-10; chart the week,
  find the dominant metric, fix its cause, re-measure. Feedback loop, one metric at a time.
- **Strategic vs tactical** — strategy splits the big goal into chunks; tactics plan one chunk's day.
- **Don't begin until ready to finish** — pick only what fits the day's real capacity; split the rest.
- **Eisenhower 1-4** on every task; delegate yellow, delete purple.
- Day planned in Google Calendar to group same-context tasks — one switch costs ~23 min.
