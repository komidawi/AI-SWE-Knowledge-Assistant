## Why

Behavioral rounds are a named failure mode alongside the design round, and there is no story bank,
so every answer is composed live under pressure. That mechanism produces exactly the observed
pattern — a good improvisation on a familiar prompt, a bad one on an unfamiliar prompt.

Shape if accepted: 8 stories, Situation / Task / Action / Result with a number in every Result,
covering conflict, a failure that was mine, ownership beyond the brief, ambiguity, influence without
authority, hardest technical problem, disagreeing with a decision, proudest shipped thing. Each
spoken aloud and timed, anything over 2:30 cut.
