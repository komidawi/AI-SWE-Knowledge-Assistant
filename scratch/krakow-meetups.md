## The actual constraint: hours

`[[2026]]` budgets 8h/week, fully allocated across two goals with ~2h total slack. One evening
meetup is ~3h door to door — more than a week's slack, and the write-up queue is 87 terms deep.

Rules to hold:

1. **Two evening events per month, maximum.** Attend on title, not on group.
2. **A meetup counts toward a goal only if the talk is on a ledger term or a course module.**
   Otherwise it is leisure — fine, but log it as leisure, not as goal hours.
3. **No meetup displaces a write-up.** The sweeps are done; only write-ups close the goal.
4. **OpenCoffee is outside this budget** — 8:30 AM, before work, costs no goal hours.
5. **Extend the standing ledger rule to talks.** Every talk attended produces same-day rows in
   `[[interviewing]]` for every term used that you could not have explained unprompted. A talk is a
   cheap interview with no consequences; the waste is not writing the rows down.
